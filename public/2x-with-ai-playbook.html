Part 1

Find Your Relevance

Before tactics, get clear on who you serve and what only you can say.

  • Who already trusts you, and why do they come back?
  • What painful problem do you solve better than generic advice ever will?
  • What can you say with proof, taste, and lived specificity?

Messaging

Who you are. What you say. Why it lands.

Specificity beats performance.
Relevance beats cleverness every time.
Presenter cue Make the audience feel the difference between sounding impressive and sounding useful.
Slide 06
Live Exercise

Mastermind Hot Seat

Before we go further, let's put it into words, out loud, in the room.

  • 01 What one unique problem are you solving?
  • 02 Who are you solving that for?
No generic answers. If it sounds like it could apply to anyone, it isn't specific enough yet.

Part 2

Build the Systems

The four systems that compound: leads, channels, giveaways, CRM.

  • Warm leads: the easiest place to recover momentum.
  • Traction channels: where discoverability becomes a habit.
  • Giveaways: value-first trust builders that earn attention.
  • CRM: the memory layer that keeps growth from leaking.
