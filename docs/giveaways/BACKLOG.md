## Why these and not others

Two findings drove the set, and they still hold:

1. **The live giveaways skew to the wrong ICP.** guardog, claude-md, cross-cli-compatibility-routing, agent-infrastructure, benchmark, compare, maccleaner and web-design-arsenal are tool-shaped. They attract the technical builder, who belongs in the book to Build With Joe lane, not the creative or spiritual service founder who buys the Mastermind. Bias new giveaways toward business-owner-shaped, not tool-shaped.
2. **The constraint is the middle of the funnel.** Twenty-six entry points, one 30-minute intro call at the end, nothing in between. Idea 3 exists to fix that and raises the value of every other row.

Each idea targets one of three axes the brand brain says move this buyer: money leaking out (sections 23 and 25), the capacity ceiling (section 1), and the fear of sounding generic (sections 2 and 25 benefit 5).
